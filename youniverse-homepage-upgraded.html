01 / Our story

A family business built around making.

Youniverse started with retail, learned through experience, and grew into a clothing business that keeps design and production close to home.

Mahesh and Amit Nishar did not start with a factory. They started with a single retail clothing shop in Thane, then opened a second in Kalyan — learning what customers wanted by being close to every sale.

In 2015, the brothers moved from selling clothes to making them. A small manufacturing unit in Vikhroli became the first chapter of Youniverse: a brand built around original design, dependable fits, and hands-on work.

From Diwali 2025, production moved into a larger dedicated space in Dadar West. The space is bigger now, but the way of working remains direct, curious, and practical.

“We started small, kept learning, and kept making. The space is bigger now, but the hands-on way we work remains.”
— Mahesh & Amit Nishar, founders
