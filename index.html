01 / Our story

A family business built around making.

Youniverse began in retail, learned through experience, and grew into a clothing business that keeps design and production close to home.

Mahesh and Amit Nishar began with a single retail clothing shop in Thane, then opened a second in Kalyan. In 2015, they moved from selling clothes to making them and Youniverse was born.

From Diwali 2025, the business moved production to a larger dedicated space in Dadar West — the current home for original prints, dependable fits, and hands-on work.

“We started small, kept learning, and kept making. The space is bigger now, but the hands-on way we work remains.”
