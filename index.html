Our Story

Two brothers, one Youniverse.

Mahesh Nishar and Amit Nishar didn't start with a factory — they started with a single retail clothing shop in Thane. It did well enough to open a second in Kalyan, each one run the same hands-on way.

In 2015, the brothers took the leap from selling clothes to making them — a small manufacturing unit in Vikhroli, and the first stitches of what would become Youniverse.

"We started small, kept learning, and kept making. The space is bigger now, but the hands-on way we work remains the same." Mahesh & Amit Nishar, Founders

From Diwali 2025, Youniverse moved production into a new, larger space in Dadar West — the next chapter for a family business built on fabric, fit and original design.

The Journal

Notes on how the clothes actually get made, from the Dadar workshop.

Design

Every Print Starts On Paper, Not On a Shelf

We don't pick prints from a catalogue — every one is designed in-house first.

Read the story
Before a Youniverse shirt exists, the print does. Our in-house design team sketches and builds every graphic from scratch — nothing is licensed off a stock sheet or copied off a trend report. Once a design is locked, we source the base fabric from manufacturers we trust across India and China, chosen for weight, weave and how well they'll hold the print. From there, everything happens under one roof: the fabric is cut, the piece is stitched, and it's finished and checked in our own Dadar unit. Keeping tailoring in-house means we can hold a size range from XS to 3XL on the same design, and adjust fit or run a custom order without waiting on an outside vendor. It's slower than just reselling someone else's stock. But it means every print you see on a Youniverse piece exists because we drew it that way.

Process

One Set or One Thousand

We size our production for a single customer and a container-load retailer the same week.

Read the story
A lot of manufacturers pick a lane: either they run bulk for wholesale, or they do small custom batches for individual customers, rarely both well. In Dadar, we do both out of the same unit. A retailer can place a large order and we'll run it at scale — we have the experience and the floor space for it, and it's most of what we do. But we've kept the line open for anyone who just wants one set, in one size, for themselves. From XS to 3XL, a single piece gets made with the same process as a bulk run. That flexibility is deliberate. Some of our best-selling designs started as a one-off request before a retailer noticed them and asked for more.

Future

From Thane to the World

Pan-India was the first chapter. Mahesh and Amit Nishar on what comes after.

Read the story
Youniverse grew out of a single retail shop in Thane, then a second in Kalyan, before the brothers built their own manufacturing unit in Vikhroli in 2015 — and, since Diwali 2025, a bigger home in Dadar. Somewhere in that move from selling clothes to making them, Youniverse became a pan-India label. "Every market we've entered so far has been because a retailer found us, not the other way around," says Amit. "The next step is doing that on purpose — outside India." Mahesh adds that the plan isn't to change how Youniverse works to get there: original designs, in-house tailoring, and the ability to take an order of any size — that's the part that travels best. For now, that means growing the wholesale side and slowly introducing the label to retailers beyond India, while keeping design and stitching in the same Dadar unit it runs from today.
