Our Story

Made in Mumbai,
made for you.

Youniverse is a family-run women's western-wear label born in the heart of Dadar, Mumbai. From ladies' shirts and jeans to denim jackets and breezy linen, every piece is crafted in-house — which means we control the quality, the fit, and the fabric ourselves.

We believe great clothing starts with great fabric. Fabric first. Always. It's why our customers keep coming back, and why every garment carries a little bit of our name.
